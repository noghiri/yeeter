About Yeeter, the Anti-social Anti-network

Yeeter is an anti-social anti-network taped together by @noghiri because I got bored. a friend who refuses to be credited here did some css and optimization stuff without me ever asking for it.

This system saves nothing. It's a pile of hand-written html files I made in Notepad++ with some css.

It throws your words into the browser equivalent of /dev/null. This means you can finally realize the social media utopia dream, including:

No followers
This means nobody's following you, nobody looking over your shoulder, no fear.
No saved posts
Other social media networks have experimented with ephemeral posting, but this is insufficiently secure - people can screenshot your posts. This solution is superior because now nobody can access the posts.
No harrassment
The lack of interaction capability means nobody can use this service to harrass you or anyone else, making this the friendliest platform.
No Nazis
This service is the first successful attempt at making a social media website that is entirely incapable of being used as a Nazi recruitment platform. No recruitment, no Nazis!
